## Interests

- Analysis of extreme precipitation events in a changing climate.
- Use of gridded data (precipitation, temperature, evapotranspiration) in hydrological studies.
- Integrated modeling of surface water and groundwater using coupled hydrological models.

## Education

- **2023** — *Diploma in Hydrology and Climate Change Impacts*, Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez, Santiago, Chile — 16 SCT credits.
- **2014–2020** — *Civil Engineering*, Universidad de La Frontera, Temuco, Chile. Graduated with highest distinction. Thesis: "Construction of High-Resolution Spatial Intensity-Duration-Frequency Curves in Central-Southern Chile".

## Professional experience

###### 2020 – Present — Consulting Engineer, ICASS Spa

- Hydrological and hydraulic projects focused on the water cycle and integrated resource management.
- Strategic Water Management Plans (PEGH) with the Dirección General de Aguas (DGA), including the Maipo basin, the Maipo–Rapel coastal basins, the Lluta and Pampa basins, and the Quebrada de la Concordia basin.
- Design of drinking water networks and support for rural drinking water projects.
- MODFLOW model for the Barahona Tailings Dam (Codelco, El Teniente).
- Technical advisory to Minera Los Pelambres on water resources management.
- Currently leading integrated modeling in the Maipo basin.

###### 2022 — Associate Researcher, ANID Project 11190864

- Study of the evolution of precipitation intensities using high-resolution satellite data and non-stationary frequency analysis, under the guidance of Dr. Violeta Tolorza.

###### 2020 — Web Developer, Universidad de La Frontera

- Development of [chi2.ufro.cl](https://chi2.ufro.cl) for the ANID/UFRO-NSF 190018 project: interactive maps, charts and data tables.

###### 2020 — Platform Developer, Universidad de Chile

- Update of the [CAMELS-CL platform](https://camels.cr2.cl): data interface, charts and Docker deployment.

###### 2020 — Research Assistant, Universidad Austral de Chile

- Participation in the FONDAP 15110009 project: "Streamflow Response to Native Forest Restoration in Former Eucalyptus Plantations in South-Central Chile" (Lara, A. et al.). [DOI: 10.1002/hyp.14270](https://doi.org/10.1002/hyp.14270)

###### 2019 — Intern, Dirección de Obras Hidráulicas

- Support for technical inspections of rural drinking water (APR) projects in Quintrilpe, Vilcún and Santa Juana Los Acacios.

###### 2016–2017 — Analyst, Observatorio Volcanológico de los Andes del Sur (SERNAGEOMIN)
